CheckKaro: Civic Document & Anti-Middleman Assistant

Democratizing Public Services • Eliminating Unofficial Bribes & Bureaucratic Friction

GitHub Repository Live Assistant Demo

In government administrative offices, common citizens frequently encounter an opaque web of paperwork, deliberately ambiguous requirements, and predatory touts/middlemen charging 10x to 50x official government fees. CheckKaro was engineered as an open, accessible citizen assistant that provides algorithmic document checklist verification, transparent legal fee disclosures, and step-by-step application walkthroughs for 50+ critical public certificates (Birth & Death, Ration Cards, Caste & Income Certificates, Land Deeds, and Old Age Pensions).

⚠️ The Systemic Problem (Middleman Exploitation)
  • Extortionate Bribery: Unregulated middlemen demand ₹1,500 – ₹5,000 for basic public documents whose statutory fee is as low as ₹20 – ₹50.
  • Information Asymmetry: Common citizens are misled about mandatory vs. optional paperwork to create artificial urgency and reliance on touts.
  • Bureaucratic Harassment: Applications are repeatedly rejected without clear guidance, forcing daily-wage workers into paying bribes.
🛡️ The CheckKaro Solution (Direct Citizen Empowerment)
  • 100% Statutory Fee Transparency: Discloses exact government challan fees, official gazette circulars, and zero-fee exemptions.
  • Algorithmic Pre-Validation: Evaluates eligibility in advance via decision tree traversal to ensure first-attempt administrative approval.
  • Right to Service (RTS) Shield: Displays legally mandated turnaround timelines and designated vigilance officer contacts.
